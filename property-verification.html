PROPERTY INTELLIGENCE

Understand the property, not just the listing.

Havenly's confidence layer is designed to make important property information easier to see, compare and question before you decide what to do next.

What the confidence score means

The score is an information-quality indicator. It reflects the completeness and confirmation status of information available to Havenly. It is not a valuation, survey, legal opinion, mortgage decision, structural assessment or guarantee that information is correct.

Core property facts

Examples include property type, location, price, bedrooms, bathrooms and tenure.

Supporting information

Examples can include floor area, EPC, parking, lease information, service charges and other relevant details.

Source & confirmation

Where available, information should be linked to an appropriate source or confirmation process rather than presented as an unexplained fact.

Information requiring verification

Important unknowns should remain visible. Buyers should verify material information with the agent, seller and appropriate professional advisers.

Why this matters in the UK

Current UK government work on material information emphasises giving consumers relevant information clearly, upfront and in time to support informed transactional decisions. The exact information needed can vary by property and transaction.

Havenly is not the final authority

  • Estate agents and other property professionals remain responsible for information they provide as part of their service.
  • Havenly does not replace conveyancers, surveyors, mortgage advisers, valuers or other regulated/professional advisers.
  • Property information can change. Always check the latest documents and confirmations before making an offer or entering a contract.
  • Demo properties and scores on this site are illustrative and should not be relied upon for a real transaction.

For agents

Havenly is designed to encourage structured listing information, confirmation workflows and visible gaps rather than hiding uncertainty. Before production use, agent verification, audit trails, source provenance, document handling, retention and operational review must be implemented.
