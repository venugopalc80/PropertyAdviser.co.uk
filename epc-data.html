PROVENANCE

A certificate is evidence, not a promise.

EPC records can show current and potential energy ratings, efficiency, floor area and other certificate information. Havenly should use them as attributable evidence and show the certificate date and source, rather than turning an EPC into a guaranteed running-cost figure.

Source: Ministry of Housing, Communities and Local Government Energy Performance of Buildings data. England and Wales coverage. The official EPC Register remains the definitive place to check whether a valid EPC has been produced for a property.
