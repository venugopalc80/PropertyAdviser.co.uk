COOKIES

Cookies & similar technologies

Current demo configuration. Havenly uses browser storage for selected demo state, including saved-property and comparison state. It does not intentionally deploy advertising or analytics cookies in this demo.

Strictly necessary technology

Technology that is strictly necessary to provide a service requested by you, maintain security or support core accessibility and functionality may be used where permitted.

Optional technology

Before production launch, every cookie and similar technology must be inventoried, including purpose, provider, duration, device access and recipients. Non-essential technologies should not be activated before the consent required by applicable rules has been obtained.

Your choices

A production service should provide an accessible preference centre, allow optional choices to be changed or withdrawn, and retain appropriate evidence of consent. You can also clear browser storage through your browser settings.

This page is informational and is not legal advice. Cookie and device-storage practices should be reviewed under PECR and UK data-protection requirements before launch.
