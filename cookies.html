This demo uses browser storage for features such as saved properties, comparisons and demo enquiries. It does not currently deploy advertising or analytics cookies.

Before production

A live UK service should identify cookies and similar technologies, explain their purposes, set retention periods and obtain any consent required before using non-essential technologies. Essential functionality should be distinguished from optional analytics or advertising.

Your choices
