Privacy notice

Demo only. This site is a concept demonstration and is not currently a production property service. The demo stores saved property IDs, comparisons and submitted demo enquiries/viewing requests in the browser's local storage.

Data used by this demo

Information entered into demo forms stays in the browser used for the demo. No agent messaging backend or production customer database is connected. Do not enter sensitive personal, financial or identity information.

Production privacy requirements

Before launch, the operator should establish and publish the controller identity and contact details, purposes and lawful bases, retention periods, data-subject rights, processors, international transfers, security measures and complaint routes. Any automated decision-support features should be clearly explained and appropriately governed.

Cookies and similar technologies

The demo primarily uses local storage rather than advertising cookies. A production service should assess all cookies and similar technologies and provide appropriate consent and preference controls where required.
