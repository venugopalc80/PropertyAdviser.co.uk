PRIVACY

Privacy notice

Demo status. Havenly is a property marketplace demonstration. This page explains the current demo behaviour; it is not a substitute for a production privacy notice.

What the demo processes

When authentication and Supabase-backed features are enabled, Havenly may process account details such as email address and profile information, plus property activity such as saved homes, enquiries and viewing requests. The service should only collect information needed for those purposes.

Why information is used

Production purposes may include creating and securing an account, providing saved-property and comparison features, handling enquiries and viewing requests, operating the marketplace, preventing abuse and maintaining security. The controller must document the applicable lawful basis for each purpose.

Sharing

Buyer requests may need to be shared with the relevant property professional so that the requested service can be provided. Production processing should identify processors, recipients and any international transfers clearly before collection.

Retention and rights

A production service should publish retention periods and provide appropriate routes for access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and other applicable UK GDPR rights, together with a complaint route to the ICO where appropriate.

Security

Authentication, database access policies, least-privilege permissions, secret management, audit logging, backups and incident-response procedures should be reviewed before real customer data is processed.

Cookies and device storage

See Cookie information. Optional non-essential cookies or similar technologies must be assessed and controlled where required.

Legal review

This demonstration is not certified as compliant with UK GDPR, PECR, the Data Protection Act 2018 or other applicable law. Obtain professional privacy and data-protection advice before launch.
